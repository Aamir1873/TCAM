# TCAM 🎞️

TCAM is a little cinematic camera experiment for the iPhone — part camera, part film lab, part excuse to make ordinary walks look like the opening scene of a movie.

It is a personal project built for fun and curiosity. Expect strong opinions about color, slightly dramatic UI, and absolutely no promise that every photo needs to look sensible.

## What it does

- Cinematic, three-strip, two-strip, and monopack looks
- 0.5×, 1×/2×, and 5×/10× lens controls
- Exposure presets from −1 EV to +1 EV
- 4:3 and 3:4 capture formats
- Full-screen preview after capture
- Optional flash and location-aware photo watermarking

Tap the shutter, pretend you are shooting on a film set, and enjoy the tiny bit of magic when the finished frame appears in the preview.

## If you want to play with it

- Xcode 26.6 or newer
- Swift 6 language mode
- iOS 26.4 or newer
- A physical iPhone is recommended for camera testing

## Run it

Open `TCAM.xcodeproj` in Xcode, select an iPhone destination, grant the camera/photo/location permissions, and run the `TCAM` scheme.

A physical iPhone is strongly recommended — the Simulator is excellent at pretending to be a phone, but less excellent at pretending to have a camera.

## Public someday

This started as a personal playground and may become a public project later. Until then, it is mostly here for experiments, nice colors, and the occasional happy accident.
